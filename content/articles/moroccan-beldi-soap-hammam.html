Beldi soap and the Moroccan hammam ritual

Beldi soap, often known as Moroccan black soap, is closely associated with the hammam tradition. Its rich texture and simple presentation fit naturally into a slower bathing ritual focused on cleansing and exfoliation.

A traditional hammam step

In a traditional hammam setting, warmth and steam create the atmosphere for a deliberate bathing routine. Beldi soap is used as part of the cleansing process before exfoliation with a kessa glove.

How the ritual flows

A simple sequence can be: warm the skin, apply the soap according to the product instructions, rinse, and then use a kessa gently if appropriate for your skin. The emphasis is on measured pressure rather than aggressive scrubbing.

Choosing a product

Choose a clearly labelled cosmetic product and follow its directions. Traditional names can be used for different formulations, so checking the ingredients and intended use is more useful than relying on the name alone.

Make the hammam ritual your own

The modern version does not need to be elaborate. A warm shower, a comfortable towel and a few considered products can capture the slower spirit of the ritual without trying to reproduce an entire hammam.
