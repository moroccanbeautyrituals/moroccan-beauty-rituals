# Moroccan Beauty Rituals — Editorial Content Audit

_Audit date: 2026-09-21_

## Current strengths

- The site has a clear visual identity, a functioning article collection, no broken internal links, and useful landing pages for Ingredients, Wedding Day and Henna Day.
- Argan Oil and Ghassoul have the strongest existing editorial foundations, including practical sections, FAQs and discreet affiliate disclosures.
- The existing image library already covers the core rituals and gives the publication a consistent warm, Moroccan-inspired visual language.

## Articles to deepen first

- **Henna, Moroccan Hammam, Rose Water and Sidr Hair Care** are currently very short (roughly 137–149 words of body copy) and read more like introductions than magazine articles.
- **Henna Day, Wedding Day and Beldi Soap** are also too brief for their search intent. Henna Day and Wedding Day have clear potential as editorial hubs rather than single short guides.
- **Argan Oil and Ghassoul** are the strongest articles, but both need canonical URLs. They can also anchor more deliberate links into the Ingredients and Hammam hubs.

## Cluster opportunities

- **Argan:** strengthen the relationship between the main ritual, hair use, skin use, the Ingredients hub and the wider Moroccan beauty routine.
- **Hammam:** connect the hammam, beldi soap, ghassoul and rose water through clear ritual steps and an at-home interpretation that avoids medical claims.
- **Henna:** distinguish cosmetic natural henna from unspecified temporary products; connect the base ritual to Henna Day and Wedding Day without treating every use as bridal.
- **Henna Day / Wedding:** develop their distinct search intents. Henna Day can cover atmosphere, preparation and decor; Wedding Day can focus on calm beauty preparation and links to bridal hammam and henna content.

## Repetition and linking

- Several supporting articles repeat the headings “Continue exploring Moroccan beauty rituals” and “A considered next step.” Future updates should use topic-specific closing sections.
- Internal links work, but the newer pages and less-developed articles have low incoming-link counts. Topic hubs should link to cornerstone articles, and cornerstone articles should return readers to their relevant hub.

## SEO and technical notes

- Every article has one H1, a title, a meta description and a descriptive primary image alt text.
- `argan-oil.html` and `ghassoul.html` are missing canonical URLs. The homepage and privacy page also do not yet carry canonical metadata.
- Current URLs and filenames should be retained. No broken internal links were found during this audit.

## Editorial priorities

1. Deepen Hammam, Henna, Henna Day and Wedding Day as the next cornerstone cluster.
2. Add canonical URLs where absent and refine internal links as pages are updated.
3. Expand Rose Water, Beldi Soap and Sidr as supporting guides with distinct practical angles.
4. Consider a separate Henna Night decor article only after the Henna Day hub is deep enough to avoid overlapping content.
