Celebration & ritual

The Moroccan henna day

A Moroccan henna day can bring together preparation, hospitality and celebration. The ceremonial setting, henna designs and the time shared with loved ones give the occasion meaning beyond a beauty routine.

A ceremonial setting

The details of a henna day vary, but a thoughtfully prepared space can create a memorable atmosphere. Textiles, candles, tea and ceremonial objects can be part of a welcoming setting without prescribing a single way to celebrate.

Explore with care

Henna has cultural meaning as well as a decorative role. When using cosmetic henna, choose products with clear ingredient information and follow their directions carefully.
